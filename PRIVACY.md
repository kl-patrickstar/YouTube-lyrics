# Privacy Policy — YouTube Lyrics

## Data Collection
This extension does NOT collect any personal data.

## Local Storage
All settings, cached lyrics, and offsets are stored locally
in your browser via `chrome.storage.local`. Nothing is sent
to our servers (we don't have any).

## Third-Party APIs
The extension fetches lyrics and artwork from:
- LRCLIB (https://lrclib.net)
- lyrics.ovh (https://lyrics.ovh)
- iTunes Search API (https://itunes.apple.com)

These requests contain only the artist/title of the song
being viewed. No tracking, no analytics, no third-party scripts.
